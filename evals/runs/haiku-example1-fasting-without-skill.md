## How far can this data be trusted?

**✅ High reliability:**
- Data from the official API of the Wikimedia Foundation (the organisation behind Wikipedia)
- Automatic objective statistics, not subject to manipulation
- All views from all IP addresses are counted

**⚠️ Limitations and caveats:**
1. **Mobile traffic**: The data includes all device types, but mobile traffic is growing everywhere
2. **Robots and bots**: Automated bots are excluded, but some background scripts may remain
3. **Caching**: Some views may not be counted due to browser caching
4. **Seasonality**: Interest in diets depends on the season (New Year's resolutions in January-February)
5. **Competition**: There may be a shift to other information sources outside Wikipedia

**Reliability verdict**: **The data is 90-95% reliable** for analysing popularity trends within Wikipedia itself, but does not reflect overall interest in intermittent fasting on the internet (where blogs, YouTube, TikTok and specialised sites may dominate).
