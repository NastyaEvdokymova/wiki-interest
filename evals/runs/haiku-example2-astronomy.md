## Result: interest in astronomy is **declining**

The news is not good. Here is what the Ukrainian Wikipedia data shows for the last 2 years:

**Main trend:**
- 📉 Interest is **falling by 50% per year** (~9.7 views per million edition views)
- **Confidence: high** (p-value = 0.0001): this is a statistically significant decline, not chance
- Over the last 12 months views fell by **45.5%** compared with the previous year

**Details:**
- Current activity: ~559 article views per month
- **There is seasonality**: peaks in September–December (evidently tied to the school year), but it does not offset the overall decline

**Why can this data be trusted?**
✓ The trend was detected with high statistical significance  
✓ The period is long enough (24 months) to rule out short-term fluctuations  
✓ Seasonality was taken into account

**Important limitations:**
⚠️ **Views ≠ demand for a course**: this is only a signal of attention to the topic, not a measure of willingness to pay  
⚠️ Part of the decline may be due to the global drop in Wikipedia traffic (AI search intercepts queries)  
⚠️ These are readers from different countries, not necessarily from Ukraine

**Conclusion for you:** Adding an astronomy course right now looks risky: interest is falling. It may be worth considering specialised topics or checking demand through a survey, not only Wikipedia.
