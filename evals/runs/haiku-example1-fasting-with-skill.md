## Analysis results

**To your main question: no, interest is NOT growing; it is declining SIGNIFICANTLY.**

### Key figures

| Metric | Value |
|---------|----------|
| **Trend over 3 years** | ↓ 39% per year (significant decline) |
| **Year over year (last 12 months)** | ↓ 47.7% |
| **Current views** | 3.04 per million edition views |
| **Monthly average** | ~183 views |

### How far can the data be trusted?

**Confidence: medium.** The tool does not say "high", for three reasons:

1. **Small base**: 183 views/month is a small volume that is sensitive to noise
2. **Strong seasonality**: amplitude 1.03, with clear peaks in January, April, August and September, which makes it harder to isolate the real trend
3. **Outliers**: a notable spike on 14 April 2025 (245 views in a day), but it does not cancel the overall trend: the decline is statistically significant (p < 0.0001)

### Important limitations

- **Views ≠ demand**: this is a signal of attention to the topic, not people's willingness to pay for or buy intermittent fasting services
- **Bot traffic**: some views are bots that the filter does not fully catch
- **Global Wikipedia trend**: since 2024 overall Wikipedia views have been falling (competition from AI answers in search); the data is normalised per language edition, which partially but not fully compensates for this

### Conclusion

On Czech Wikipedia, interest in intermittent fasting has **clearly been falling** over the last 3 years. This can be trusted: the decline is statistically significant, but the data has a medium confidence level due to the small number of views and seasonal swings.
